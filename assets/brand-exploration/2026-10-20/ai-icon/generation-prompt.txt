Use case: logo-brand.
Create ONE finished proprietary AI feature icon for Typeflux, a premium macOS productivity assistant. This is a small in-interface symbol, not an application tile or presentation.
Design concept: "Flux Spark". A compact near-square continuous flowing ribbon forming a softly rounded four-lobed diamond silhouette, with ONE very clear four-point spark cut out of its center as genuinely transparent negative space. The ribbon has a subtle forward-flow asymmetry, a distinctive folded-ribbon crossover at the lower right, and chunky generous thickness. The central spark aperture occupies about one third of the mark width so it remains legible at 16–24 pixels. Four softly pointed outer corners, smooth concave transitions, expertly controlled simple geometry. It should suggest intelligence and inspiration at a glance, and feel related to a flowing infinity ribbon without being an infinity sign.
Color: rich saturated turquoise at upper left flowing into cobalt blue at lower left, violet at bottom and right, with a restrained coral-pink accent at upper right. Continuous clean restrained gradients, never pale or washed out. Strong silhouette equally readable on white and dark gray.
Style: immaculate mostly flat vector-like icon with subtle color depth; no 3D extrusion, no glossy plastic, no texture, no cast shadows, no glow. One cohesive shape, no detached satellite stars, no tiny details, no letters, no text, no eyes, no robot, no surrounding circle, no app-tile background. Do not resemble Gemini's simple solid star, Google multicolor G, or OpenAI knot.
Composition: one centered icon on a square canvas, mark fills 84% of width and height with balanced transparent margins. Actual alpha transparency outside AND in central spark cutout. Deliver only the isolated icon, no mockup, no labels, no grid.
