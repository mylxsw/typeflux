a-spark-bud
Use case: logo-brand. Create ONE standalone colorful AI feature icon for Typeflux, a premium macOS productivity assistant. Concept A: "Spark Bud". Three chunky tapered rounded ribbon petals grow upward from a single shared base, the central petal taller and gently tilted, the two side petals sweeping outward. Their negative space forms a broad tiny spark-like notch near the heart. The overall shape is a compact abstract sprout of intelligence / emerging inspiration, not a literal botanical illustration, not a flame, not a lotus. Simple memorable silhouette and substantial filled areas for 16–32px use. Saturated turquoise left petal, rich cobalt-to-violet central petal, magenta-to-coral right petal. Carefully controlled smooth gradients, mostly flat vector-like finish, precise soft curves. On a genuinely transparent square canvas with 10% margins. One isolated mark only. No text, no lettering, no app tile, no badge, no surrounding circle, no shadow, no glow, no glass, no thin strokes, no disconnected tiny decorative elements. This should feel like a proprietary AI function symbol, not a full app icon. Strong contrast on white and near-black. Render clean anti-aliased edges with no stray pixels.

b-dialogue-spark
Use case: logo-brand. Create ONE standalone colorful AI feature icon for Typeflux, a premium macOS productivity assistant. Concept B: "Dialogue Spark". A near-square softly rounded speech-bubble outline made from ONE thick smooth flowing ribbon, with a short integrated speech tail at bottom left. A single large four-point sparkle floats in the transparent center of the bubble. The sparkle is solid violet with a subtle coral highlight. The bubble ribbon transitions smoothly from saturated turquoise on upper left to cobalt on bottom left to purple and pink on right. A bold simple readable speech-plus-intelligence symbol, precisely balanced for use at 16–32 px. Bubble band thickness roughly 17% of total mark width, generous transparent spacing around the central sparkle. Clean largely flat vector-like graphic, gently softened corners. Genuinely transparent square canvas, mark occupies 82% of width, no opaque fill in bubble. One icon only, no text, no letters, no app tile, no shadows, no glow, no glass, no 3D extrusion, no tiny details, no extra stars. Strong on light and dark backgrounds. Beautifully controlled geometry and clean alpha edges.

c-flux-knot
Use case: logo-brand. Create ONE standalone colorful AI feature icon for Typeflux, a premium macOS productivity assistant. Concept C: "Flux Knot". A compact fluid four-way ribbon knot with a rounded square overall footprint, made of TWO broad interlocking curved S-shaped ribbons at right angles. The ribbons create four rounded lobes and one small but clearly open diamond aperture at center. Express connected intelligence and continuous flow. It should read as a single confident abstract mark at 16–32 pixels. No intricate weaving, at most two visible over-under junctions, substantial color areas, center opening about 22% of mark width. One ribbon transitions from turquoise to cobalt; the other from violet to warm coral pink. Clean mostly flat vector-like treatment, no harsh gradients, exact smooth curves, a little asymmetric forward motion. Not the OpenAI six-loop knot, not a flower, not an infinity sign, not a four-point star silhouette, not Microsoft Copilot logo. True alpha transparency outside and through all openings. Square canvas, centered with 10% clear margins. Only one icon; no text, no border, no background tile, no glow, no shadow, no glossy material, no tiny details. Saturated colors that remain readable on white and near-black.
