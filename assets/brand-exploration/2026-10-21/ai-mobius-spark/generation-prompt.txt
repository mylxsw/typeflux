Use case: logo-brand / compositing.
Design ONE new proprietary Typeflux AI feature icon by thoughtfully fusing the TWO supplied references.
Reference 1: the user's black AI icon, an open rounded-square frame with a large four-point sparkle in its upper-right opening and a smaller sparkle inside. Use its clear square footprint, open-corner composition and immediately recognizable AI sparkle language.
Reference 2: Typeflux's actual app logo, a flowing infinity / Mobius ribbon. Use its continuous strip, two-loop flow and unmistakable over-under twist as the brand DNA. DO NOT copy its dark app tile, glossy material or pale rainbow colors.
Create a SYNTHESIS, not separate logos pasted together: one broad electric-blue ribbon forms a compact diagonal Mobius/infinity loop rising from lower left toward upper right. Its generous lower-left lobe has a softly squared outer contour, recalling the rounded-square enclosure from reference 1. At the upper-right lobe the ribbon seamlessly opens out into a prominent four-point inspiration spark, with an acid-lime face/segment forming the spark's upper-right side. The folded blue strip flows through a single unmistakable over-under crossover near the middle-lower part and returns to the rounded lower-left lobe. Both the lower-left loop opening and the sparkle/upper-right opening are transparent negative space. The whole silhouette should feel almost square, roughly as tall as wide, not a wide horizontal infinity symbol. The spark and ribbon must form ONE coherent designed mark with elegant clear geometry. Keep the reference's SMALL secondary sparkle only if it can be expressed as a clean generous negative-space opening within the lower-left loop; no extra floating decorative stars.
Palette: dominant saturated electric blue #3148E8 and one restrained acid-lime accent #C8EE55, about 80/20. No cyan-purple-pink rainbow gradients. Mostly flat filled color, only minimal same-hue shading at the ribbon crossover to communicate which part passes over. No glass, shine, glowing edges, cast shadows, fine outlines or texture.
This is a small toolbar AI icon, so use thick simple forms, broad openings and few parts that survive 20–32 px. No text, no letters, no circle badge, no rounded-square background tile. Expertly smooth vector-like edges, no stray pixels or fringe. Actual transparent background outside and through the holes. One isolated centered icon on square canvas with 10% clear margin. Deliver the icon only, no labels or mockup.
