## Dark ribbon icon extraction

```text
Use case: background-extraction. Asset type: Typeflux macOS app icon master. The supplied image is the exact selected third thumbnail: a glossy ivory infinity ribbon on a near-black rounded-square tile, with a subtle violet glow at the crossover. Remove ONLY the black canvas outside the rounded-square tile and make that exterior genuinely transparent. Keep the tile and everything inside it visually unchanged: exact infinity geometry, luminous ivory highlights, silver shading, violet crossover glow, dark graphite tile gradient, scale and centered placement. Clean smooth antialiased rounded-square edge. Do not redraw, simplify, recolor, crop any part of the tile, or add text or decoration. Output one isolated icon on a transparent square canvas.
```
