# Typeflux

**Less switching. More flow.**

An AI productivity app for Mac. Launch apps, find information, type with your voice, capture your screen, and recover what you copied—all within reach.

[Download Typeflux](https://github.com/mylxsw/typeflux/releases/latest) · [Website](https://typeflux.app) · [Watch the demo](https://youtu.be/ZxWWUOEgaJ4)

macOS 13+ · Apple silicon & Intel · Free and open source

## Say it. See it at your cursor.

Write emails, reply to messages, or capture an idea: hold your voice shortcut, speak, and release to insert the text into your current app. See a live transcript as you talk. Select existing text and say “make this shorter” or “translate into English” to revise it in place.

Choose different writing styles for work emails and casual conversations. Pick where voice input appears, too:

## Think it through with Chat

Start a conversation from the launcher with a file, screenshot, or selected text. Ask AI to research, organize information, translate, or write. It can also work with files in folders you authorize and operate apps to carry out a task.

A built-in browser, conversation history, and notebooks keep your research and work together. Save recurring tasks as instructions or workflows, then call them with a keyword next time.

## Make it yours

- **Shortcuts and appearance:** customize shortcuts and keywords, choose light or dark mode, and pick an interface style.
- **Local or cloud:** run speech recognition offline on your Mac, or choose a cloud service. Use Typeflux Cloud or your preferred AI provider.
- **You control permissions:** by default, AI asks before changing files or running commands.

The Typeflux app is free and open source. Cloud services may incur charges. When you use a remote model, the voice or text you submit is sent to your chosen provider.
